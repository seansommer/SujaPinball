Original real-time sound design
Continuous speed-sensitive ball rolling, solenoid body, rubber impacts,
spring pull, metallic overtones, spinner ratchets, ingredient bells,
target-bank reset, orbit/combo stings, cold-press pump, bottle fizz,
Power Press, kickback, ball-save, tilt, and jackpot layers are synthesized
by the game. They require no media-generation service during play.

Music, effects and cabinet callouts have independent volume controls.
Backgrounding or pausing immediately stops music, callouts, rolling and
scheduled effects. Player recordings are not sent to an audio service.
