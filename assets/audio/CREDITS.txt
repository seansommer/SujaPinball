SUJA PINBALL! — music, samples and voices

All external recordings below were released by their creators under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Original pinball chimes, impact layers, ramp sweeps and musical reward fanfares
are synthesized by this game's Web Audio engine. Music defaults to 17%,
effects to 80%, and voices to 85%; music ducks while a voice is playing.
