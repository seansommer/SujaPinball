# SUJA PINBALL! Art Bible

Direction: a polished, luminous juice-factory pinball cabinet. Emerald lacquer,
chrome, lime-green flippers, violet conveyor lighting, sculpted fruit bumpers,
translucent juice, and a giant SUJA bottle that fills and caps as a live toy.

Original illustrations were generated with OpenAI's built-in image-generation
tool for this project. The PNG masters are retained here; optimized derivatives
serve the game. No Runway images or video are used. Original audio provenance is in audio/README.md.

## Generation briefs

Cover: original premium commercial arcade pinball key art, a luminous chrome
ball with a rainbow trail, a translucent SUJA bottle, fruit pop bumpers,
conveyor ramps, and two lime flippers in an emerald-and-violet juice factory.
Exact title “SUJA PINBALL!” and subtitle “SUJA GAME CENTER”. Landscape 3:2,
with a quieter lower-left area for real menu controls.

Portrait cover: recompose the landscape world for a vertical phone screen,
title at the top, bottle and pinball action in the middle, and a quiet dark
apron at the bottom for the live mode selector and Press Start button.

Icon: use the cover only as a style reference. A large chrome ball, green SUJA
bottle, orange and berry bumpers, and two luminous lime flippers; strong
square silhouette and no title, border, rounded corners, or fake UI.

Playfield: a flat, orthographic printed texture with dark emerald lacquer,
delicate metallic botanical artwork, lime and purple arcade curves around
the perimeter, dark open center, and a restrained sunburst/leaf emblem below.
No physical bumpers, balls, flippers, ramps, bottles, shadows, or text.

The playable table is modeled in code. Bumpers, flippers, rails, ramps, bottle,
juice level, caps, lighting, particles, and ball trails remain live geometry
and effects, rather than painted-in game parts. The compatibility renderer
uses the same physical layout and game state.
